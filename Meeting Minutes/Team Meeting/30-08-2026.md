### Topic 2: Data Analysis and Standardisation
  - Four members will work on data analysis and job data standardisation.
  - The team agreed on the following initial standard data columns:

    - `job_title`
    - `job_description`
    - `job_url`
    - `salary`
    - `location`
  - `salary` and `location` will be collected when the information is available.

  - The team reviewed API endpoints and identified that equivalent fields may have different names across companies.
  - The team will investigate an AI-assisted approach to map different API field names to the standard column names.
  - Non-English job postings will be translated into English using AI while retaining the original text where possible.
  - `company_name + job_title` will initially be used as a temporary identifier.
  - The team will review a more robust primary key strategy after examining the collected data.
