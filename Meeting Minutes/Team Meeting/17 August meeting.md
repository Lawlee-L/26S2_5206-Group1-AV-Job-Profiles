## Discussion
### Deliverable 1 framing and stakeholders:
- The team reviewed the motivation and problem statement. The central problem was framed as the gap between autonomous-vehicle (AV) industry requirements and curriculum development, together with the absence of a centralised AV jobs resource for students.
- The problem statement should cover both intended outcomes: information for the client developing curriculum and an AV job-search website for students.
- The deliverable should distinguish the primary client-facing outcome from the student-facing outcome and consistently refer to the report/dashboard and website.

### Scope, MVP fields and terminology:
- For the MVP, the virtual organisation chart supplied by the client should be explicitly mentioned, along with the agreed report fields.
- Date posted, salary, generic salary, location and required skills were identified as optional for the MVP but required for the fuller report where the data is available.
- Education requirements and experience levels were treated as later extensions rather than MVP content.
- The two outputs were named the “AV Industry Insights Dashboard” for the client and the “AV Job Search Website” for students.

### Requirements and data workflow:
- Functional requirements should be written in a consistent, testable “The system should …” form and should cover system processing as well as user-facing functions.
- Non-functional requirements should be prioritised and measurable; data accuracy was discussed as a clearer example than an undefined response-time promise.
- The workflow should show job data collection, cleaning and processing, classification/analysis, structured outputs, and delivery through the dashboard and job-search website.
- The student job-search website uses the collected and cleaned job listings; the client dashboard uses the classified and analysed data for industry insights.

### Classification approaches and AI acknowledgement:
- The project plan should name the candidate classification approaches precisely: rule-based classification, semantic clustering and LLM-assisted classification/analysis.
- Browser automation and manual collection were removed from the MVP resources list. ATS/API collection remains the current MVP approach, with HTML parsing retained as a possible later method depending on the company list.
- The final deliverable should separately acknowledge how AI was used in developing project materials, including meeting-minute summarisation, in addition to describing AI/LLM use within the product.

### Project plan, testing and deployment:
- The work breakdown should separate evaluation of job/skill classification from testing of the completed dashboard and website.
- The plan should explicitly include both outputs, with the AV Industry Insights Dashboard before the AV Job Search Website, followed by testing and final delivery.
- The weekly plan should show dashboard work in week 8 and job-search website development in week 9.
- The deployment architecture was not finalised. The team noted possible UWA IT integration and a static hosted job website refreshed approximately every two weeks; the client-facing analysis/report deployment remained unresolved.

### Submission workflow:
- The submission should link to the GitHub repository and relevant project resources, including meeting notes. Meeting records can be numbered and cited in the report’s references.
