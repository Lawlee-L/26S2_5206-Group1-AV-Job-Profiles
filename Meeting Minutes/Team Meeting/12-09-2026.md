  - Li Luo suggested removing unrelated jobs first to reduce dataset size before further analysis; Sunjol agreed this is reasonable given the data volume.
  - Sunjol will upload the non-LLM pipeline today, and the LLM-based and posting pipelines by Monday once polished.

  - Primary key decision: keep a unique source key (platform + company + source ID) combined with an auto-generated ID as the primary key; Li Luo confirmed the source key is unique across all job postings.
