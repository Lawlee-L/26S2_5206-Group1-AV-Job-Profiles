## Discussion
### Topic 1: Project Purpose and Users
  - Adrian explained that the project will mainly be used by him and may also be shared with students in the future.
  - The project should help users understand:
    - what types of jobs are available in the AV industry;
    - what skills are commonly required; 
    - how job opportunities and skill requirements change over time.

### Topic 2: Report and Dashboard
  - Adrian would like the team to prepare a report based on the collected job data.
  - He would also like a dashboard website that students can use to explore the data.
  - The report and dashboard should include:
    - job titles;
    - required skills;
    - company names;
    - current and previous job advertisements; 
    - links to the original job advertisements or company careers pages.
  
### Topic 3: Historical Job Data
  - Adrian would like the team to collect previous job advertisements where possible.
  - This will help users see how job demand, job titles and skill requirements have changed.
  - Links to the original advertisements or careers pages should be kept where available.

### Topic 4: Automatic Updates
  - Adrian would like the job data to be updated automatically.
  - He said that either daily or weekly updates would be acceptable.
  - The team has not decided on the final update frequency.
  - The decision will be made after the team investigates the available data sources, collection methods and technical difficulty.  

### Topic 5: Using AI to Analyse Jobs
  - Adrian explained that different companies may use different titles for similar jobs.
  - Companies may also introduce new job titles even when the responsibilities are similar to existing roles.
  - He would like the team to investigate using AI to:
    - read and analyse job descriptions;
    - identify what type of job each advertisement represents;
    - group similar jobs together;
    - assign a common job title;
    - identify commonly required skills; 
    - recognise new or emerging roles.
  - The main goal is to produce a clear list of common job titles and common skills across the AV industry.  

### Topic 6: Reference Website
  - Adrian provided a reference website to show the team some relevant ideas.
  - The team will review the website and decide which features may be useful and possible to include in the project.
  - **Reference website:**
    - https://aijobs.net/
    - https://www.levels.fyi/zh-cn/companies/waymo/salaries
### Topic 7: Initial Company Research
  - Adrian provided a list of approximately 41 AV companies.
  - The companies have been divided among the six team members.
  - Each team member will:
    - find the careers page for their assigned companies;
    - check how the companies publish their job advertisements;
    - find out whether an API is available;
    - investigate other collection methods if there is no API;
    - check whether current and historical job data is available;
    - record the relevant website links;
    - note any problems or restrictions.
  - The team will compare the findings and decide which data collection methods are suitable for the project.
## Key Decisions
**Decision 1:** The project will be designed for Adrian and may also be shared with students.  
- The main outputs will be a dashboard report and a website.
- The project will focus on AV job opportunities and required skills.

**Decision 2:** The team will collect both current and historical job data where possible.  
- The team will aim to collect data from approximately the past two years.
- Original job advertisement or careers-page links will be kept where available.

**Decision 3:** The job data should be updated automatically.  
- Adrian is happy with either daily or weekly updates.
- The team will decide on the update frequency after completing the initial research.

**Decision 4:** The team will investigate an AI-assisted method for analysing job descriptions.  
- Similar jobs with different titles should be grouped under a common job title.
- Common skills should be identified for each type of job.

**Decision 5:** The initial research tasks were assigned.  
- The list of approximately 41 AV companies was divided among the six team members.
- Each member will investigate the careers pages and data collection options for their assigned companies.
