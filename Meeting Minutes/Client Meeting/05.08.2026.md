Discussion
Topic 1: Initial Company Research and Data Collection
The team provided an update on the initial research conducted on the autonomous vehicle (AV) companies provided by the client.
The team identified that different companies use different career platforms and website structures, meaning that the appropriate data collection method varies between companies.
Some websites also present additional challenges because their career pages are available primarily or exclusively in languages other than English. In particular, two Chinese companies were identified whose career pages and available job opportunities are mainly focused on China. This may limit the geographical scope and accessibility of the job data collected from these companies.
The team requested further guidance from the client regarding how to proceed with companies where data extraction is difficult or where relevant AV positions are limited.
________________________________________
Topic 2: Scope of Autonomous Vehicle Jobs
The team discussed the types of jobs that should be considered relevant to the project.
Some companies do not advertise positions explicitly labelled as autonomous vehicle jobs but instead advertise related engineering roles, such as positions involving chip development and other technologies that may contribute to autonomous vehicle systems.
The client clarified that the project should focus on jobs that are relevant to autonomous vehicles.
The scope may include jobs associated with different types of autonomous vehicles, including:
•	On-road autonomous vehicles.
•	Off-road autonomous vehicles.
•	Autonomous vehicles used within the mining industry.
•	Relevant positions within subsidiaries or sub-companies of the selected organisations.
The client will provide additional information to clarify the required job categories and types of autonomous vehicles that should be included in the project.
The client will also provide a Virtual Organisation (Virtual Org) to assist the team in understanding the required project scope and relevant vehicle categories.
________________________________________
Topic 3: Company Selection and Geographic Scope
The team discussed companies that have presented difficulties during the initial data collection process.
A list of companies for which data extraction is difficult will be provided to the client. The client will review the list and advise whether these companies should remain within the project scope.
The client specifically identified Bosch as a required company for the project. For Bosch, the team should primarily focus on job opportunities available in:
•	Germany.
•	United States of America.
The client confirmed that where a large organisation operates through subsidiaries or sub-companies, it is acceptable for the team to collect relevant jobs from those entities.
The team also noted that career searches may sometimes redirect to or display jobs under different company or subsidiary names. These cases should be considered when identifying relevant AV jobs.
________________________________________
Topic 4: Data Collection Strategy
The team discussed the order in which companies should be approached for data extraction.
The client recommended starting with companies that have the easiest and most reliable data collection methods before progressing to companies with more difficult website structures.
Applicant Tracking System (ATS)-based websites were identified as an appropriate starting point because they generally provide more structured job data and are easier to extract.
The team will initially prioritise Greenhouse ATS websites before progressing to other ATS platforms and more difficult extraction methods.
Where a company's career platform contains many unrelated jobs, the collected data can be filtered so that only jobs relevant to autonomous vehicles are retained.
________________________________________
Topic 5: Required Job Data Fields
The client clarified the information that should be collected for relevant job advertisements.
The required data fields include:
•	Advertised job title.
•	Generic/virtual job title.
•	Date posted.
•	Job location.
•	Salary, where available.
•	Skills required for the position.
The distinction between the advertised job title and the generic/virtual job title should be maintained so that different job titles can potentially be grouped into common career categories.
Additional requirements regarding relevant job types will be provided by the client.
________________________________________
Topic 6: AI and Job Classification
The use of AI services for processing and classifying collected job information was discussed.
The client suggested experimenting with models such as Claude.
AI may potentially assist with tasks such as interpreting job advertisements, identifying relevant AV positions, extracting skills, and mapping advertised job titles to generic/virtual job titles.
This functionality can be explored as part of the development of the project's MVP.
________________________________________
Topic 7: Data Storage, Caching and Historical Jobs
The client discussed the need to retain job information collected by the system.
The developed system should cache job advertisements retrieved from company career websites rather than relying only on currently available job postings.
Previously collected jobs should remain stored so that historical job information can be accessed even after the original job advertisement has been removed from the company's website.
The team should investigate an appropriate approach for storing and archiving historical job data. Archive.org was mentioned during the discussion as a possible reference when considering historical web content.
The website can initially be developed and tested using a local server. Hosting infrastructure for the final developed website will need to be considered as the project progresses.
