## Discussion

### Topic 1: Progress of Company Data-Source Investigations

- Team members reviewed the investigations of their assigned AV companies and the findings recorded in individual GitHub issues and the shared Excel tracker.
- Many careers websites use a recruitment platform or Applicant Tracking System (ATS), such as Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby, and expose reusable structured endpoints.
- A common collection script may be reused for companies on the same ATS by changing the company identifier.
- Some sources still require further investigation, HTML parsing or browser automation.
- API responses use different field names and formats. Some job descriptions are returned as HTML, so tags and irrelevant content must be cleaned before analysis.
- The team needs a common data structure for fields such as company, original job title, location, job description, responsibilities, requirements and job URL.

### Topic 2: Proposed MVP Company and Geographic Scope

- The team discussed prioritising companies with reliable and accessible job data for the MVP.
- Large, diversified companies may contain many jobs unrelated to autonomous vehicles and will require stronger filtering.
- Some Chinese careers websites and regional recruitment systems may be difficult to access or interpret consistently.
- The team discussed potentially focusing the MVP on companies with accessible ATS or API data, while retaining more difficult sources for later investigation.
- The final company list, geographic scope and definition of an AV-relevant role must be confirmed with the client. No final MVP scope was approved during this meeting.

### Topic 3: Historical Data

- Most careers sources do not provide complete historical job data.
- The team discussed beginning regular snapshots of current job postings so that historical comparisons can be built over time.
- Whether historical data is required for the MVP remains to be confirmed with the client.

### Topic 4: Data Cleaning, Classification and Technical Workflow

- The team identified classification as a central technical challenge after data collection and cleaning.
- Job titles differ between companies, so the project will need agreed labels for common job titles, job families and skill categories.
- The client should clarify which departments and role types are in scope, including software, electrical, mechanical and other technology-related roles.
- Nyx presented the proposed staged workflow documented in GitHub Issue #1: collect data, clean and normalise it, pre-classify it with scripts or dictionaries, use an LLM to assist classification, and generate the dashboard and report.
- The team discussed the trade-off between a paid online LLM API and a locally hosted model. The client should clarify expected resources, budget and performance requirements.
- The team discussed working on classification labels in parallel with the remaining API, HTML-parsing and browser-automation investigations.

### Topic 5: IP, Copyright and Data Usage

- The team noted that public job listings may still have terms of use, copyright and data-usage considerations.
- The proposed public-facing portal or dashboard should not be finalised until the permitted collection, storage and presentation of source data has been considered and discussed with the client.

### Topic 6: Communication and Meeting Arrangements

- All members were asked to check Microsoft Teams and GitHub regularly and keep their investigation records up to date.
- The shared Excel tracker will be used as a consolidated view, while detailed evidence and findings will remain in GitHub issues.
- The client liaison will contact the client to request availability and ask whether the next client meeting should be online or in person.
- The regular facilitator meeting schedule is not yet finalised. Dr Sumayyah will confirm the course expectation with Bruce and update the team.

## Key Decisions

Decision 1: Company investigation findings will be consolidated in the shared Excel tracker, with detailed evidence maintained in individual GitHub issues.

Decision 2: The team will prepare a proposed MVP around manageable, accessible data sources, but the company list, geographic scope and eligible role types remain subject to client approval.

Decision 3: The team will define a common data structure and cleaning process before combining data from different ATS and careers websites.

Decision 4: Classification research will proceed in parallel with the remaining data-extraction investigation. The classification labels and LLM approach must be discussed with the client before they are finalised.

Decision 5: Because complete historical data is generally unavailable, the team will consider collecting dated snapshots from the current period onward. The MVP requirement for historical analysis remains pending client confirmation.
