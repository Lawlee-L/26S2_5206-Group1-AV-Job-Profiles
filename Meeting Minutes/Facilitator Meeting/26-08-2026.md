## Discussion

### Topic 1: Project Progress and Current Stage

- Dr Sumayyah asked the team about the current progress of the project.
- The team confirmed that the project is progressing.
- The project is currently focused on data collection, data processing and classification.
- The team discussed the next steps for processing the collected job data and progressing towards the development stage.

### Topic 2: Data Cleaning and AV Job Relevance

- The team discussed that not every job advertised by an AV-related company is necessarily relevant to the AV field.
- The collected jobs need to be checked to determine whether they are relevant or irrelevant to the project.
- Irrelevant information in job descriptions should be removed before further processing.
- The team will investigate different methods for cleaning and classifying the job data.

### Topic 3: Multilingual Data Processing

- Some collected job information may be available in languages other than English.
- Multilingual job titles and descriptions may need to be translated into English before further processing.
- A consistent method is required so that data collected from different companies and regions can be analysed together.

### Topic 4: Skill Extraction and Standardisation

- The team discussed extracting skills from relevant job descriptions.
- Skills may appear in different parts of job advertisements, including requirements, qualifications, responsibilities and descriptions.
- The same skill may also be written in different ways across different companies.
- The team discussed the need to standardise extracted skills.
- Possible methods using Python and LLMs will be investigated and tested.

### Topic 5: Skill Structure and Soft Skills

- The team discussed whether skills should be organised using a main skill–subskill structure.
- The team also discussed whether soft skills should be included in the final skill classification.
- No final decision was made about including soft skills.
- The team agreed to ask the client, Andrian, whether soft skills should be included.
- Andrian will also be asked whether a main skill–subskill structure is suitable and how flexible the classification system should be.

### Topic 6: Weekly Data Collection and Historical Tracking

- The team discussed collecting job data regularly to track changes over time.
- Nyx will run the data collection script once a week.
- Each dataset will be saved with a different collection date.
- The datasets can then be compared to identify newly added jobs.
- This process will help the team build historical job data during the project.
