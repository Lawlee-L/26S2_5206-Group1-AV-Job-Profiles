- Delayed client approval may affect the report.

- Inaccessible project resources may affect the supporting evidence.
- Inconsistent writing or formatting may reduce the quality of the report.
- Last-minute PDF conversion or submission issues may cause delays.
- Unclear responsibility for submission may result in a missed or duplicate submission.
