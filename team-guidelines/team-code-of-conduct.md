# Team Code of Conduct

**Status:** Draft (To be refined throughout the project)  
**Last Updated:** 9 August 2026

## 1. Goals and Philosophy

- Establish a collaborative and respectful team environment in which all members can actively participate and contribute.
- Encourage open communication, knowledge sharing, and constructive feedback.
- Ensure that every team member has an equal opportunity to participate in discussions and decision-making.
- Promote shared accountability and mutual support throughout the project.

## 2. Team Roles and Leadership Rotation

Team roles have been assigned to support effective communication, coordination, and accountability.

### 2.1 Team Leadership

The Team Lead role will rotate among at least three members during the project:

1. **Li Luo** will serve as the first Team Lead until approximately Week 5 or Week 6.
2. **Nyx Chen** will serve as the second Team Lead.
3. **Seonjeong Jeong** will serve as the third Team Lead.

The exact leadership handover dates may be adjusted according to project progress and team needs. To ensure a smooth transition, the outgoing Team Lead should brief the incoming Team Lead on current progress, outstanding tasks, risks, deadlines, and important decisions.

The Team Lead is responsible for:

- Coordinating team activities and meetings.
- Monitoring project progress and deadlines.
- Supporting task allocation and follow-up.
- Maintaining task allocation records and the meeting minutes rotation schedule.
- Encouraging all team members to participate actively.
- Identifying project blockers and escalating them when necessary.
- Ensuring that important plans, decisions, and changes are documented.
- Uploading a blank weekly work summary document to the Microsoft Teams **Team – Shared** folder for all members to complete.
- Reminding members to complete their assigned sections and confirming that all sections have been completed.
- Compiling the completed document and uploading the final weekly work summary to the **CITS5206 Channel** by 7:00 PM every Sunday.

### 2.2 Client Liaison

**Sunjol Singh Paul** will serve as the primary Client Liaison.

The Client Liaison is responsible for:

- Acting as the main point of contact between the team and the client.
- Coordinating client meeting times and related arrangements.
- Sending necessary meeting information and team documents to the client.
- Ensuring that client requirements, feedback, and requested changes are documented.
- Assisting the team in obtaining client confirmation of the project requirements, MVP, and other deliverables.

Questions for client meetings will be prepared and discussed collectively by the team. Meeting decisions and outcomes will also be jointly documented and followed up by the team.

### 2.3 Facilitator Liaison

**Li Luo** will serve as the primary Facilitator Liaison.

The Facilitator Liaison is responsible for:

- Acting as the main point of contact between the team and the facilitator.
- Confirming facilitator meeting times and related arrangements.
- Sending the agenda prepared by the team before each facilitator meeting.
- Sending the minutes prepared by the assigned minute-taker after each meeting.
- Communicating necessary team information or changes to meeting arrangements to the facilitator.

All team members will share responsibility for participating in discussions, asking questions, recording relevant information, and completing follow-up actions arising from facilitator meetings.

### 2.4 Observers and Supporting Members

**Thushamini Chathusika Hewa Pathegamage** and **Leon Nel** will act as observers during relevant meetings and provide support when required.

Their responsibilities may include:

- Observing and recording key discussion points.
- Identifying issues, risks, or missing information.
- Assisting with documentation and meeting preparation.
- Supporting other members when workload or skill gaps arise.
- Providing feedback to support team decision-making.

These roles do not restrict any member to a single area of work. All members are expected to make meaningful contributions and may take on additional or rotating responsibilities throughout the project.

## 3. Communication

- Microsoft Teams will be the primary communication channel for team discussions and updates.
- Team members should regularly check team messages and respond within 24 hours where possible.
- Important decisions, meeting outcomes, and project progress should be documented through GitHub.
- Team members should communicate blockers or possible delays as early as possible.
- If a team member cannot respond or complete a task within the agreed timeframe, they should notify the team and discuss alternative solutions.
- Relevant communication with the client and facilitator should be shared with the team to maintain transparency and continuity.

## 4. Meetings

- Regular team meetings will be held every Saturday from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM.
- Meeting agendas should be prepared before scheduled meetings where possible.
- Every team member will take turns preparing meeting minutes during the project. Completed minutes will be stored in GitHub.
- The minute-taking schedule can be found in the **CITS5206 Meeting Minutes Rotation** document in the **Meeting Minutes** folder.
- Attendance and absences should be recorded in the meeting minutes.
- If a team member is unable to attend a meeting, they should notify the team in advance.
- Members who miss a meeting should watch the meeting recording or review the meeting minutes and follow up on relevant decisions and assigned action items.
- Every member should prepare in advance and participate actively in meetings.

## 5. Project Planning and Task Management

- The team will follow an Agile development approach throughout the project.
- Tasks will be assigned according to project priorities, individual skills, member responsibilities, and team capacity.
- Project progress, deadlines, responsibilities, task dependencies, and milestones will be tracked using shared project management documents and GitHub.
- Team members are responsible for completing their assigned tasks within the agreed timelines and regularly updating their task status.
- Every team member is responsible for completing their weekly work summary over the weekend.
- The weekly summary document can be found in the Microsoft Teams **Team – Shared** folder.
- Each member should complete their assigned section with a brief summary of the tasks they undertook, the work they completed, and their current progress.
- All members should complete their sections in time for the Team Lead to upload the completed document to the **CITS5206 Channel** by 7:00 PM every Sunday.
- If a member expects that they will be unable to complete a task or their weekly summary on time, they should notify the Team Lead as early as possible and explain the reason.
- Project plans should be reviewed and adjusted when project requirements, risks, or team capacity change.
- Where possible, a backup team member should be identified for important tasks.

## 6. Decision-Making and Conflict Resolution

- The team will aim to make decisions through discussion and consensus.
- Different opinions should be considered respectfully before a final decision is made.
- Important decisions and the reasons for them should be documented.
- If the team cannot reach a consensus, the Team Lead may organise a team vote or propose a practical resolution.
- If a disagreement cannot be resolved internally, the team will evaluate the available options and seek guidance from the facilitator or client where necessary.
- Before accepting a change requested by the client, the team should evaluate its advantages, disadvantages, risks, resource requirements, and effects on the project scope and deadlines.

## 7. Review and Updates

- This document will be reviewed periodically throughout the project.
- Team members may suggest updates to improve collaboration and respond to changing project requirements.
- Any significant changes to team roles, responsibilities, meeting arrangements, or decision-making processes should be agreed upon by the team and formally documented.
