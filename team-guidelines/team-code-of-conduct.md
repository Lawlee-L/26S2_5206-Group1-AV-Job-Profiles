# Team Code of Conduct

**Status:** Draft (To be refined throughout the project)  
**Last Updated:** 05 August 2026

## 1. Goals and Philosophy
- Establish a collaborative and respectful team environment where all members can actively contribute.
- Encourage open communication, knowledge sharing, and constructive feedback.
- Ensure each team member has equal opportunities to participate in discussions and decision-making.

## 2. Communication
- Microsoft Teams will be the primary communication channel for team discussions and updates.
- Team members should regularly check team messages and respond within 24 hours where possible.
- Important decisions, meeting outcomes, and project progress should be documented through GitHub.
- Team members should communicate blockers or delays as early as possible.
- If a team member cannot respond or complete a task within the agreed timeframe, they should notify the team and discuss alternative solutions.

## 3. Meetings
- Regular team meetings will be held every Saturday from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM.
- Meeting agendas should be prepared before scheduled meetings when possible.
- Meeting minutes will be recorded by rotating team members and stored in GitHub.
- Each team member is expected to prepare meeting minutes at least once during the project.
- If a team member is unable to attend a meeting, they should notify the team in advance.

## 4. Planning and Task Management
- The team will follow an Agile development approach throughout the project.
- Tasks will be assigned based on project priorities, individual responsibilities, and team capacity.
- Progress, deadlines, and milestones will be tracked using shared project management documents.
- Team members are responsible for completing assigned tasks within agreed timelines.

## 5. Decision-Making and Conflict Resolution
- The team will aim to reach decisions through discussion and consensus.
- Different opinions should be considered respectfully before making final decisions.
- If disagreements cannot be resolved, the team will evaluate available options and seek guidance from the facilitator or client when necessary.

## Review
- This document will be reviewed periodically throughout the project.
- Team members can suggest updates to improve collaboration and adapt to changing project requirements.
